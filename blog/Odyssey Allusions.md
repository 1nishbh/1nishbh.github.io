## The communication and the collective

allusions are great tool in literature and more so in poetry. what wonder it must be that writers need more than the subject they are writing about to drive the lessons home and full. we all have felt something similar to this in our own life -- that insatiating, irksome feeling of so many things left unsaid, miscommunicated, under-expressed, etc. this def calls for an invention of a great writing tools: metaphor, simile, allusions, even short punches of sentences. Or even god -- what is god after all but our sole seeking for something greater, further from human limitations?

allusions, in particular, are really interesting to me because they build upon our collective consciousness. we refer to, and in doing so preserve, our collective past while alluding to historical events, individuals, or myths. especially good poems bridge the gap of the time and space while alluding to events of distant times. this brings our ancestoral endeavors and lives next to us, making us realize how we are walking the same threads centuries later.
